## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Brazilian men who buy premium menswear for how it signals competence and taste without shouting — business owners, executives, lawyers, doctors, brokers, and other liberal professionals. Secondary: grooms and event guests sourcing a standout blazer for a wedding or formal occasion. All are men who evaluate fabric, cut, and presence over logos, shopping from São Paulo and nationwide Brazil.

## Product Purpose

MadeinItaly Outlet (madeinitalyoutlet.com.br) is a curated online outlet selling premium Italian men's tailoring — blazers cut from noble Italian mill fabrics, imported directly and sold at a meaningful discount (up to 60%) versus full-price haute couture tailoring. Success is a completed purchase through one of two live, operating channels: the e-commerce store (madeinitalyoutlet.com.br) or direct WhatsApp sales (+55 11 97836-5191).

## Positioning

"Outlet" here means curatorial opportunity, not clearance liquidation: limited, hand-picked lots of finished blazers from historic Italian weaving houses (Vitale Barberis Canonico, Loro Piana, Reda), offered at outlet pricing without lowering the material or construction standard. The claim a competitor selling generic "Italian-style" blazers could not truthfully copy is the direct, named-mill sourcing plus the discount-without-compromise positioning.

## Operating Context

- Sales happen through exactly two channels, both confirmed live today: the e-commerce storefront at madeinitalyoutlet.com.br, and WhatsApp for stock/size questions and reservation of pieces.
- There is **no physical showroom, atelier, or in-person appointment/consultation service.** The site's copy and recent commit history (`refactor: substituir termo Boutique por Outlet`, `fix: remover referencias a alfaiate e focar exclusivamente em vendas de produtos do outlet`) deliberately removed bespoke-tailor/consultant/"atelier booking" framing in favor of pure ready-to-wear retail language. Do not reintroduce booking-a-consultant, made-to-measure, or "alfaiate" (tailor) framing without an explicit request.
- A fit-guidance quiz on the site (3 questions: occasion, cut preference, color palette) recommends a specific in-stock blazer and routes the visitor to WhatsApp or the e-commerce store — it is a merchandising/conversion tool, not a bespoke consultation.
- Free size exchange within 30 days; up to 12x interest-free installment payment; national shipping across Brazil.
- Instagram presence: @madeintalysp.

- Real historic facts about partner mills are established and reusable: Vitale Barberis Canonico founded 1663 (oldest continuously operating wool mill), Loro Piana & Reda heritage dated 1924.

## Product Principles

1. Outlet pricing is a curatorial advantage, never a discount/liquidation tone — copy and design should never read as a clearance sale.
2. Every product claim (mill, composition, size, price advantage) must be real and verifiable; nothing is invented to fill space.
3. Quiet luxury over ostentation — restraint in color, materials, and Italian motif usage is a brand requirement, not a stylistic default to be overridden casually.
4. The purchase path is always one of exactly two channels (e-commerce or WhatsApp) — no other contact/booking mechanism should be implied.
5. Presence and craft do the persuading; the site should let material and cut carry the argument rather than declarative superlatives.
